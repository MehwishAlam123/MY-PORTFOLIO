Knee MRI Abnormality Detection Model (Research)

Project Overview & Innovation

View Knee MRI Research Repo

Knee injuries such as ACL tears, meniscus abnormalities, and chondral defects require highly accurate assessment from multi-slice MRI scans. This project develops a multimodal deep learning system designed to detect 12 specific knee abnormalities simultaneously.

12 Target Classes

ACL tear, PCL rupture, Medial meniscus tear, Lateral meniscus tear, Chondral defect, Bone marrow edema, Joint effusion, Patellar tendinopathy, and more.

Multimodal Input

Direct ingestion of 3D DICOM pixel arrays coupled with tokenized radiology report notes for weak-supervision and multi-label classification.

Weak-Labeling

Automated extraction of training labels from unstructured medical text reports to overcome scarce annotated training datasets in clinical settings.
